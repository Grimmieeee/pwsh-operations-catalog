## Scope

- Natural investigation queries such as suspicious login, unexpected MFA prompts and compromised account lead to IR Risk Assessment. Explicit action searches retain their intent.
- IR keeps required input visible; detailed CHECKS/GUARDS remain available in one disclosure. Preconditions, first-sign-in workflow permissions and readiness-tool access appear before download.
- One primary IR package downloads the three exact-version verified scripts, a workflow guide and their SHA-256 manifest. Individual script/source controls remain available separately.
- The browser assembles the ZIP only after all three sources pass existing integrity checks and share one release version. Corrupt source, missing verification or mixed versions block the package.
- START shows extraction, existing-window execution and a path-prompted launch command. The shared launch helper also applies to the 80 downloadable scripts, without assuming a current directory or bypassing policy.
- Each IR step shows its expected result, next objective, and blocked/cancelled path. Containment is conditional; recovery readiness is reviewed after containment; account restoration requires approval; sign-in with MFA and 48-hour monitoring remain follow-up work.

The remaining library retains its source-backed checks and guards. This release does not invent per-tool success criteria or claim client runtime testing for unverified scripts. Missing sources remain pending. No published PowerShell bytes or verification evidence change.
